Earnings

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026

Earnings is a read-only viewer for your own Google AdMob and AdSense reporting data. There is no server. Your earnings data travels from Google directly to your device and is never sent anywhere else. We cannot see it, because it never reaches us.

What the app accesses

When you connect your Google account, Earnings requests two read-only permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adsense.readonly

Read-only means exactly that: the app can read your reports and cannot change anything in your AdMob or AdSense account. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What is stored, and where

WhatWhereLeaves your device?
Google sign-in tokeniOS KeychainNo
Your most recent earnings reportApp container, shared with the widget so it can display themNo
Your settings — currency, theme, thresholdsOn-device preferencesNo

Signing out deletes the sign-in token. Deleting the app removes everything above.

Advertising

Earnings displays ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. That SDK — not this app — collects an advertising identifier and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads. This is Google’s collection, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Before the first ad loads, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. Declining is fully supported: ads continue to work, served non-personalised, and nothing else in the app changes.

Your AdMob and AdSense earnings figures are never used for advertising and are never shared with any advertiser.

What we don’t do

  • No account with us — there is nothing to create and nothing to delete.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of our own.
  • Nothing sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No profiling, and no use of your earnings data for any purpose other than showing it to you.

Limited Use disclosure

Earnings’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through those APIs is used solely to display your own reporting inside the app, is never transferred to anyone except as required to provide that feature, and is never used for advertising or sold.

Children

Earnings is a tool for people who publish apps and websites. It is not directed at children and collects nothing knowingly from them.
