Earnings is an independent iOS app that displays your own Google AdMob and AdSense reporting data. By using it, you agree to what follows.

What you need

A Google account with an AdMob or AdSense publisher account. Earnings shows only data that account already has; it does not create, alter or delete anything in it.

The figures are estimates

AdMob and AdSense report estimated earnings and finalise them days later. Numbers shown in Earnings come straight from Google’s reporting APIs and carry the same caveat. Do not rely on them for accounting, tax or any financial decision — Google’s own reports and payment records are the authority.

Alerts are a prompt, not a verdict

Earnings flags patterns that often indicate a problem — an unusually high click-through rate, requests that go unfilled, a placement showing nothing. These are heuristics meant to tell you where to look. They are not a determination that anything is wrong, and the absence of an alert is not a guarantee that everything is fine.
