Earnings

Know when your ads break.

Earnings opens on a sentence, not a spreadsheet. “Everything looks normal” — or what isn’t, and which app it’s in.

It watches for the things a total hides

  • A placement that stopped showing ads — requests going out, nothing coming back.
  • Requests that are barely being filled, which is usually a setup problem, not demand.
  • An app clicking far above the rest of your account — what accidental clicks look like before anyone tells you.

You find out from a notification, not by going looking.

The numbers, when you want them

Today, yesterday, last 7 or 30 days, this month, last month — each compared against the period before it, so you see direction and not just a total. Break down by app, ad unit or country. Earnings, impressions, clicks, requests, match rate, show rate, eCPM and RPM.

A widget in three sizes, including the lock screen, refreshes on its own. If the app is doing its job, you’ll rarely open it.

Built small and private

There is no server. Your figures go from Google to your phone and nowhere else. Access is read-only — the app can read your AdMob and AdSense reports and cannot change anything. Nothing is uploaded, shared or sold. See the privacy policy.
