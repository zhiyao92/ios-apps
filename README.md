## [Beam: Learn Thai & Speak It](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/beam-learn-thai-speak-it/id6480093061)

Inspired by my marriage to a Thai woman and my desire to deeply understand Thai culture, Beam takes you from your first สวัสดี to holding real conversations. It teaches the thing most language apps skip — the five tones — with visual pitch guides, minimal-pair drills, and ear training. Speak a phrase out loud and Beam scores your pronunciation with on-device speech recognition, then puts it to work in role-played scenarios: ordering food, taking a taxi, bargaining, checking into a hotel.

*Previously released as Bo - Learn Thai.*

![Beam: Learn Thai & Speak It](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhiyao92/ios-apps/main/Screenshots/Beam.png)


## [Bondify: The Couples Edition](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bondify-the-couples-edition/id6761910667)

Bondify is a couples toolkit built around one idea: the right question beats small talk. Eight curated card decks — from Lovers and Deep Talk to lighter, playful sets — give you prompts for a first date, an anniversary, or an ordinary evening that deserves better than scrolling. Play together on one device or over SharePlay, and save the reflections worth keeping.


## [Whirl: Decision Roulette](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/whirl-decision-roulette/id6760835509)

Stop overthinking, start spinning. Whirl is a decision wheel and random picker that settles "what do you want to eat?" in one tap. Beyond custom wheels, it doubles as a restaurant picker: spin through top-rated places near you and see ratings, photos, and the details that actually decide it.


## [Odin Finance: Loans & Budget](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/odin-finance-loans-budget/id1538554089)

Odin turns money decisions into clear visuals. Home, car, personal, and refinance loan tools sit alongside compound interest, savings goals, ROI, budgeting, debt-to-income, emergency fund, and retirement calculators — each with a breakdown and a chart, so you can see what a decision actually costs before you make it.
