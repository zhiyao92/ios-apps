This Privacy Policy explains what information our applications collect, how that information is used, and the choices you have about it. It applies to every iOS application, watch app, widget, App Clip, extension, and related service (each, an “App”) published or operated by Zhi Yao Tan (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

It covers all of our Apps, current and future, whatever their name or subject matter, and any renamed or rebranded version of an App. Not every section applies to every App — each App only collects what its own features actually need, and the App Store privacy label on each App’s listing describes what that specific App collects. By downloading or using an App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

The short version. We do not ask you to create a named account. We do not sell your data. Advertising and tracking practices depend on the App and your choices; Tessera’s practices are described in Section 2.13. Most of our Apps use a random installation identifier so your in-app activity (such as favorites and history) stays with you. Deletion controls and retained records depend on the App, as described below.

Each App is published by Zhi Yao Tan, an independent developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. You can reach us at kelvin.vins@gmail.com for any privacy-related question or request.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on which App you use and which features you choose to use. The categories below are the complete set across our whole portfolio; an individual App will typically use only some of them.

2.1 Installation identifier

When you first launch an App that stores data, we generate a random UUID and store it in your device’s Keychain. This identifier is used for app functionality, including associating in-app activity (for example favorites, history, or purchase status) with your installation. It is not your real name, email, phone number, or Apple ID. In Tessera, the same identifier associates signing limits and RevenueCat purchase entitlements and can survive deleting and reinstalling the App, as described in Section 2.13. The statement that we do not link the identifier to an external identifier applies to Apps other than Tessera.

2.2 In-app activity and content

Depending on the App, we may store the following on our servers (Google Cloud Firestore) or on your device, under that anonymous identifier:

  • Items you mark as favorites, bookmark, or save.
  • Recently viewed content (history) and progress or streak data.
  • Content you create inside the App (for example custom decks, notes, lists, templates, saved calculations, or entries).
  • Reflections, recaps, or journal-style entries you choose to save.
  • Free-trial or opt-in unlock entitlements and their expiry.
  • Anonymous, aggregate feedback you submit (for example a thumbs up or thumbs down, a rating, or a report).
  • Preference flags such as theme, language, font, or accessibility settings, generally stored locally on your device.

2.3 Diagnostic and performance data

We use Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Analytics) to record crashes, errors, and aggregate usage information. This may include your device model, iOS version, app version, language, country, coarse region, time zone, and the in-app screens you visited. We use diagnostic and usage information to operate and improve the App. Tessera’s separate advertising and tracking practices are described in Sections 2.9 and 2.13; the statement that diagnostic information is not used to advertise to you applies to Apps other than Tessera.

2.4 Purchase information

If you buy a subscription or other in-app purchase, payment is processed by Apple. Apps that use Adapty Inc. receive purchase and entitlement information through Adapty. Tessera uses RevenueCat for purchase verification, entitlement management and purchase analytics, as described in Section 2.13. Depending on the App and purchase, this information can include product identifiers, transaction details, country, renewal status and whether an entitlement is active. We never receive your payment-card details or your full Apple ID.

2.5 Location (only in Apps with location-based features)

Some Apps offer features that depend on where you are — for example finding places near you. If you grant location permission, your approximate or precise coordinates are used at the moment of the request and sent to our mapping and places provider (Google Maps Platform) to return nearby results. Location is used to fulfil your request; we do not build a location history, track your movements in the background, or use location for advertising. You can decline location access, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, and the App will continue to work with reduced functionality (for example, by letting you enter an area manually).

2.6 Microphone and speech (only in Apps with speech features)

Some Apps let you speak so the App can check your pronunciation or accept voice input. If you grant microphone and speech-recognition permission, audio is captured only while you are actively using that feature. Recognition is performed using Apple’s Speech framework; where on-device recognition is available it is used, and where it is not, Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not store your recordings on our servers and we do not use them for any purpose other than returning your result.

2.7 Photos, camera, and other device permissions

An App will only ask for a permission (such as photo library access to save an exported image, or the camera to scan a code) when a feature you have chosen to use requires it, and the reason is shown in the iOS permission prompt. Declining a permission only disables that feature. We do not access your contacts. Health data is covered separately in Section 2.12.

2.8 Notifications

If you grant permission, an App may send local notifications (for example a daily reminder), and some Apps may send push notifications for content updates. For push notifications, Apple issues a device token that is stored with your anonymous identifier so we can deliver the message; it does not tell us who you are. You can revoke notifications at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications.

2.9 Advertising data (only in Apps that show ads)

Some Apps may display advertisements, including optional rewarded video ads, from Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested we present Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK). Whether ads are requested and how they are personalised depends on the App, your consent choices and provider requirements. Tessera may request App Tracking Transparency permission and, when authorised, use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for applicable advertising tracking. Google’s consent choices and Apple’s tracking permission are separate. The no-ATT/no-IDFA statement applies to Apps other than Tessera. See Section 2.13 for Tessera’s controls and data handling.

2.10 Shared and multi-device sessions (only in Apps that support them)

If you start a SharePlay or similar shared session, your device exchanges a small amount of session state (such as which item is currently visible) directly with the other participants’ devices through Apple’s GroupActivities framework. We do not see or store the contents of those messages. If an App syncs data through iCloud, that data is stored in your own Apple account under Apple’s privacy policy, not on our servers.

2.11 Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we keep it for as long as needed to handle your request and to keep a record of it.

2.12 Health and fitness data (Nourish only)

Nourish can read a small amount of data from Apple Health so that parts of your health picture fill in without you logging them by hand. This applies to no other App, and it only happens if you tap “Connect Apple Health” and then grant permission in the system prompt.

  • What we read: sleep analysis, exercise minutes, workouts, and mindful sessions.
  • Read-only. Nourish never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • It stays on your device. Health data read from Apple Health is used on the device to work out how your Sleep, Movement and Mind pillars are doing. It is never uploaded to our servers, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone.
  • Never used for advertising. We do not use health data for advertising, ad targeting, analytics, profiling, or any other use-based data mining, and we never disclose it to advertising networks or any other third party.
  • Declining costs you nothing else. Every other part of the App keeps working; those pillars simply show as not tracked.
  • You can revoke it at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health. Nourish stops reading immediately.

Separately, the habits you log inside Nourish yourself — water, movement minutes, meals and your own habits — are stored with your anonymous identifier so they survive reinstalling and sync between your devices, as described in Section 2.2. Those are your own entries in the App, not data read from Apple Health.

2.13 Tessera: cards, Wallet passes and advertising

Tessera recognizes card text and barcodes on your iPhone. When you create a Wallet pass, the selected card details and any chosen logo are sent over HTTPS to Tessera’s signing service, hosted on Google Cloud, to generate and sign the pass. If you choose a photo-reference pass, the confirmed cropped image is sent only after you agree in the app. Tessera removes the source image’s metadata and limits the image’s size before transmission. The original image retained for editing is not sent to the signing service. A photo-reference pass is a personal reference, not an official credential or a copy of NFC access.

After you save a photo card, Tessera keeps the confirmed image and, after an accepted edit, a bounded original for further editing in protected private files on your iPhone. These image files are excluded from device backups. Deleting the card removes its associated local images. A pass added to Apple Wallet contains the selected artwork and details. Removing a card from Tessera does not remove a separately saved Wallet pass; manage those passes in Wallet.

The signing application processes card content to return the pass and is designed not to save that content or include it in its application logs. Google Cloud also processes technical request and security information. Operational logs currently have a 30-day configured retention period and required audit logs have a 400-day retention period. These technical records are separate from saved card photographs; this is not a promise that no technical data is retained.

Tessera generates a random installation identifier stored in the iPhone’s Keychain. It can survive deleting and reinstalling the app. The signing service uses it with random card identifiers to enforce the free-card allowance, and RevenueCat uses the same identifier to manage purchase entitlements. These records can be associated with the same installation. Deleting a local card or the app does not reset this server-side allowance or delete purchase records. The current service does not automatically expire the allowance ledger.

Apple processes purchases. Tessera uses RevenueCat to verify purchase and entitlement status and analyze purchase activity; Tessera does not receive your payment-card details. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process app-usage and diagnostic information to help operate and improve Tessera. Tessera’s own analytics events do not include card photos, card text or barcode content.

The free version may show Google AdMob ads. Tessera uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to gather choices where required and checks whether ads may be requested. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen it in Tessera Settings. Tessera may request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Granting permission allows applicable advertising tracking, including use of the advertising identifier; declining does not grant that permission. Google’s consent choices and Apple’s tracking permission are separate. Ad availability and personalization depend on your choices and applicable platform requirements. Advertising services may process device identifiers, advertising and interaction data, and approximate location inferred from technical information. Tessera does not request location permission for this advertising processing. Tessera Pro removes ads.

You can delete saved cards and their local images in Tessera, remove Wallet passes separately in Wallet, and change camera or tracking permission in iOS Settings. Tessera does not currently provide the “Delete account & data” control described for other apps. Contact kelvin.vins@gmail.com for a privacy request concerning retained service or purchase records; we may need information to identify the applicable installation or transaction. We do not promise that uninstalling alone erases Keychain, service, purchase or Wallet records.

RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/ . Google’s privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy . Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ .

7. Data retention

For Apps other than Tessera, we keep in-app activity for as long as your install exists, or until you delete it from inside the App. Tessera’s local images, persistent allowance ledger, purchase records and separate Wallet copies follow the retention details in Section 2.13. Diagnostic and crash data are retained according to the applicable Firebase service’s retention practices. Firebase Crashlytics generally retains crash reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion from live and backup systems. Google Analytics user-level and event-level retention depends on the property’s settings; standard aggregated reports are not subject to those same retention limits. Purchase and entitlement records are retained for as long as needed to honour your subscription and to meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle and document your request.

Because we do not hold personal data tied to your real identity, we generally cannot identify you outside the App, and we may be unable to act on a request we cannot verify. You may nonetheless contact us at kelvin.vins@gmail.com and we will do our best to help you exercise any rights granted to you by applicable law (such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or Malaysia’s PDPA), including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
